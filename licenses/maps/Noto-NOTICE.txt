Noto map glyph attribution

The bundled PBF glyph files are unmodified copies of the Noto Sans Regular
multiscript glyph set distributed by OpenFreeMap:
https://tiles.openfreemap.org/fonts/Noto%20Sans%20Regular/{range}.pbf

OpenFreeMap identifies Noto Sans as licensed under SIL Open Font License 1.1:
https://github.com/hyperknot/openfreemap/blob/main/LICENSE.md#fonts

The PBF fontstack identifies the 23 Noto font families listed below. Their
original font copyright and license records are retained from the corresponding
OpenMapTiles source font files. The original font files are not bundled.
https://github.com/openmaptiles/fonts/tree/master/noto-sans

All of these source fonts identify SIL Open Font License 1.1 in their license
metadata. The full license is included in Noto-OFL.txt and NotoCJK-OFL.txt.
Noto-OFL.txt also retains the current Noto Project Authors copyright notice.
